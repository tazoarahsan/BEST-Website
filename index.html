How sunlight becomes power you can use

Every system we build follows the same path. Here’s what each part does.

  1. Solar panels

    Turn sunlight into DC electricity.

  2. Inverter

    Converts DC into AC, the power your building runs on.

  3. Battery storage

    Stores extra power for nights and outages.

  4. Your building

    Runs lights, appliances and machines.

  5. The grid

    Takes your surplus, recorded by a net meter.

Behind the scenes, every system also includes mounting structures, DC cabling and surge protection, charge controllers, switchgear and meters, remote monitoring, and transformers for large sites.

Ways to work with us

Turnkey build (EPC)

We engineer, supply and build the system. Once it’s commissioned, you own it outright.

Power purchase (PPA)

We own and run the system on your site. You buy the electricity at a fixed, lower rate with no upfront cost.

Maintenance contract (O&M)

Scheduled cleaning, inspections and repairs that keep your plant producing at full output.
